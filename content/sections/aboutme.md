I am an assistant professor in management and strategy at [HKU Business School](https://www.hkubs.hku.hk/), with a joint appointment in economics, and a courtesy appointment at [HKU School of Governance and Policy](https://sgp.hku.hk/). 

I am also the founding director of [Hong Kong Future Economy Institute](https://hkfei.org.hk/) (HKFEI), an independent think tank I established to bring rigorous economic research to bear on Hong Kong's policy challenges. 

I study what people actually do inside institutions — and why the gap between policy intent and actual outcome is almost always larger than designers expect. Much of my work is rooted in Hong Kong, where the density of policy intervention in housing and labor markets makes it an ideal laboratory for these questions. But the same logic extends further: to outsourcing markets in Brazil, to platform currencies in Toronto, to the organizational economics of how firms coordinate work. The thread running through my work is an interest in how formal structures interact with the informal behavior they quietly produce.

I am affiliated with [Rockwool Foundation](https://www.rfberlin.com/author/mbwonghku-hk/), [HKU Org Econ](https://www.orgecon.hku.hk/), [HKU CCCW](https://cccw.hku.hk/), [HKU Jockey Club ESG Research Institute](https://hkujcesgri.hku.hk/), and [HKU Real Estate Lab](https://relab.hku.hk/).
