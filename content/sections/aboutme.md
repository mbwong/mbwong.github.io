I am an assistant professor in management and strategy at [HKU Business School](https://www.hkubs.hku.hk/), with a joint appointment in economics, and a courtesy appointment at [HKU School of Governance and Policy](https://sgp.hku.hk/). I study how institutions shape behavior — and why the gap between what a policy intends and what it actually produces is so often larger than its designers expected.

Much of my work is rooted in Hong Kong, where the density of policy intervention in housing and labor markets makes it a remarkable laboratory for these questions. But the same logic extends further: to outsourcing markets in Brazil, to platform currencies in Toronto, to the organizational economics of how firms coordinate work. The thread running through all of it is an interest in how formal rules interact with the informal behavior they quietly produce.

I am a founding director of the [Hong Kong Future Economy Institute](https://hkfei.org.hk/) (HKFEI), an independent think tank I established to bring rigorous economic research to bear on Hong Kong's policy challenges. I am also affiliated with [Rockwool Foundation](https://www.rfberlin.com/author/mbwonghku-hk/), [HKU Org Econ](https://www.orgecon.hku.hk/), [HKU CCCW](https://cccw.hku.hk/), [HKU Jockey Club ESG Research Institute](https://hkujcesgri.hku.hk/), and [HKU Real Estate Lab](https://relab.hku.hk/).
