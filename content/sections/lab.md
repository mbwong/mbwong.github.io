My research interests are: 

1. *Organizational economics*: Developing general equilibrium models of relational contracting networks to study the structure of markets and hierarchies;
1. *Urban and labor economics*: Measuring the impact of housing on inequality and welfare (e.g. using quantitative housing assignment, housing ladder, and spatial equilibrium models);
1. *Monetary economics*: Developing models of money as a medium of exchange to make sense of currency transaction data from the field.
